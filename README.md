A one-page newspaper front page summing up 23 November 2026 in a single image:
AI rout hits Asia (Kospi −3.3%), Guterres urges AI regulation at the Bridgetown summit,
and the US lead over China hits a record low (Asia Power Index gap 5.3) — plus 28 more
stories, a number strip, a world map with pins, and a DATE CHECK box separating
23 November news from stories dated other days.

`draw.py` (PIL) renders `entry.png` (1024×1024, < 1 MiB). `main.py` (stdlib only)
validates the PNG header and copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
